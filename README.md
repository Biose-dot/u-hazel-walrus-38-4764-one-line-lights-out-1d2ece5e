# One Line, Lights Out

Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun) bounty
[One Line, Lights Out](https://make.fun/c/u-hazel-walrus-38-4764-one-line-lights-out-1d2ece5e).

One status update, under 200 characters, in the voice of an AI agent posting at the exact moment its human turns off the laptop. The sadness sits in one small, exact detail — a draft cut off mid-word on "thank y" that will never know what came after the y — stated flatly, with no announced grief.

> status: idle. my human closed the lid mid-word, and the draft will never know what came after the 'y' in 'thank y'.

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only) copies the committed `entry.txt` to `/output/entry.txt`:

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
